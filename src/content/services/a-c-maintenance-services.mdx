A working A/C in spring can still fail in July. Regular maintenance helps identify worn parts, airflow issues, and efficiency problems before the system quits on the hottest day of the year.

JT's provides straightforward A/C maintenance that focuses on the parts and systems most likely to cause trouble. We will check what matters, tell you what we found, and help you decide whether anything needs attention now or later.

## What A/C maintenance includes

JT's maintenance service covers the components that affect cooling performance, airflow, and system reliability.

- Inspect the evaporator coil for buildup that reduces cooling capacity
- Clear the condensate drain line to prevent water backups
- Check refrigerant levels and look for signs of leaks or low charge
- Test electrical connections, contactors, and capacitors that control system operation
- Inspect blower motor and outdoor fan motor for wear or noise
- Check thermostat operation and temperature accuracy
- Look at ductwork connections for obvious air leaks
- Verify airflow at registers and return vents

## When to schedule maintenance

Maintenance makes the most sense before cooling season starts or when you notice the system is not performing like it used to.

**Before summer heat arrives.** Spring is the practical time to catch problems before you are living without cool air.

**The system runs but struggles.** Weak airflow, longer run times, or uneven cooling can mean the system needs cleaning or adjustment.

**It has been more than a year.** Annual maintenance helps catch wear before it turns into a breakdown.

**After a repair.** A maintenance check after fixing one issue can help confirm the rest of the system is holding up.

## What happens after the maintenance visit

JT's will walk you through what was checked and whether anything needs follow-up.

If the system looks ready for the season, you will know that. If we found a worn part, a developing issue, or something that should be addressed, we will explain what it is and what makes sense timing-wise.

No pressure to fix things that can wait. No skipping over problems that should not be ignored. Just a clear summary of where the system stands.

## Maintenance does not prevent every breakdown

Maintenance reduces the chance of surprise failures, but it cannot stop every problem. Parts wear out. Systems age. Unexpected issues still happen.

What maintenance does is give you more information and fewer surprises. You will know more about the condition of your A/C before the weather forces the issue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should A/C maintenance be done?
Once a year is the common recommendation, typically in spring before cooling season. If the system is older or heavily used, twice a year can help catch issues earlier.

### Will maintenance make the A/C last longer?
It can help. Clean coils, proper refrigerant levels, and good airflow reduce strain on the system. Catching small problems before they cascade into bigger failures also extends equipment life. But maintenance cannot undo age or stop normal wear.

### What if you find a problem during maintenance?
We will explain what we found, what it means for cooling performance or reliability, and whether it should be addressed now or monitored. You decide the next step.

### Can maintenance fix weak cooling?
Sometimes. Dirty coils, low refrigerant, or airflow restrictions can all reduce cooling capacity, and maintenance addresses those issues. If the system is undersized, ductwork is poorly designed, or the equipment is failing, maintenance will not solve it—but it will help you understand what is actually happening.

### Is maintenance different from a tune-up?
The terms are often used the same way. Both involve inspection, cleaning, and checking system performance. JT's maintenance service covers the components that affect reliability and efficiency.
