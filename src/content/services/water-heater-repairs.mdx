Not every water heater problem means replacement. If the unit is not leaking from the tank itself and the issue is something fixable, repair can buy you time or solve the problem outright.

JT's can look at what is happening and help you understand whether repair makes sense or whether replacement is the practical next step.

## Water heater problems worth calling about

- No hot water or water that stays lukewarm.
- Hot water runs out faster than it used to.
- Unusual noises like popping, rumbling, or hissing from the tank.
- Pilot light will not stay lit or burner will not fire.
- Water temperature swings or inconsistent heating.
- Pressure relief valve dripping or leaking fittings.
- Rust-colored water or sediment buildup affecting performance.

## What we will talk through

When you call, we will ask what the water heater is doing and how long the issue has been happening. We will also want to know roughly how old the unit is, if you know.

That helps us figure out whether this is something repairable or whether you are looking at a tank that is done.

### When repair makes sense
Repair can be practical if the tank is not leaking and the problem is something like a thermostat, heating element, pilot assembly, or valve. If the tank itself is compromised or the unit is old and showing multiple issues, replacement usually makes more sense.

### When replacement is the honest answer
If the tank is leaking, corroded, or the unit has been patched before and keeps failing, we will tell you. Replacement gives you reliability and avoids the cycle of small fixes on failing equipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if the water heater can be repaired?
If the tank itself is not leaking and the problem is with a component like a thermostat, heating element, or pilot assembly, repair is often possible. JT's will look at what is failing and tell you whether fixing it makes sense or whether the unit is done.

### What if the tank is leaking?
A leaking tank cannot be repaired. The steel has corroded and the only option is replacement. If you see water pooling around the base or dripping from the tank body, call and we will help you plan the next step.

### How long does a water heater repair take?
It depends on what needs attention. Some repairs can be handled in one visit if we have the part. Others may require ordering a component and scheduling a follow-up. We will tell you the timeline once we know what is wrong.

### Should I repair an old water heater or just replace it?
If the unit is more than ten years old and needs a repair, replacement is often the smarter path. We will talk through the age, condition, and cost so you can decide what makes sense without pressure.

### Do you work on gas and electric water heaters?
Yes. JT's handles both gas and electric tank water heaters. Tell us what type you have when you call and we will help you figure out the issue.

### Can you help if the pilot light keeps going out?
Yes. A pilot that will not stay lit can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, dirty pilot assembly, or gas supply issue. We will look at what is causing the problem and explain the fix.
