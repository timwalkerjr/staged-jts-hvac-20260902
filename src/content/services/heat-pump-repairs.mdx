Heat pumps handle both heating and cooling, which means the symptoms can be harder to read. If the system is not switching modes, the outdoor unit is icing up, or the home is not staying comfortable through the seasons, JT's can help you figure out what is happening and what repair makes sense.

## Common heat pump repair symptoms

- The system will not switch from heating to cooling or back.
- The outdoor unit is covered in ice or the defrost cycle is not working.
- The heat pump runs but the home stays cold or warm depending on the season.
- You hear clicking, grinding, or hissing that was not there before.
- The system short cycles, keeps shutting off, or will not stay on long enough to make a difference.
- Weak airflow or uneven temperatures from room to room.
- The backup heat keeps kicking on even when the outdoor temperature is mild.

## What to expect when you call about heat pump repair

We will ask what the system is doing, when the problem started, and whether it is affecting heating, cooling, or both. That helps us understand whether the issue is with the reversing valve, refrigerant levels, the defrost cycle, the outdoor unit, or something simpler like the thermostat or airflow.

If it is a straightforward repair, we will talk through timing and cost before scheduling. If the system is old or has needed repeated work, we will be honest about whether repair still makes sense or whether replacement should be on the table.

## When repair might not be the right call

Heat pumps are reliable, but they do wear out. If the compressor is failing, refrigerant leaks are recurring, or the system has needed multiple repairs in a short window, replacement might be the more practical path. We will explain what we are seeing and help you decide what makes sense for your home and your budget.

Sometimes the honest answer is to keep an eye on it and plan for replacement when the timing works better. We will not push you toward a decision that does not fit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my heat pump needs repair or replacement?
It depends on what is broken, how old the system is, and how often it has needed work. We will look at what is happening and give you a straight answer about whether repair is practical or whether replacement should be part of the conversation.

### Why is my heat pump covered in ice?
A little frost on the outdoor unit is normal in winter. If the unit is covered in heavy ice or the defrost cycle is not clearing it, that usually points to a refrigerant issue, a bad defrost sensor, or a problem with the reversing valve. We can check what is causing it.

### What does it mean when the heat pump won't switch modes?
If the system is stuck in heating or cooling mode, the reversing valve is the likely cause. That is the part that changes the flow of refrigerant to switch between heating and cooling. Sometimes it is a wiring or control issue. We will figure out which one it is.

### Can you repair heat pumps in cold weather?
Yes. Some repairs are harder when the temperature drops, but we can diagnose and fix most heat pump issues year-round. If weather is going to affect timing or testing, we will let you know up front.

### How long does a heat pump repair take?
It depends on what is wrong. Simple fixes like a thermostat issue or a tripped breaker can be handled quickly. Reversing valve work, refrigerant leaks, or compressor problems take longer. We will give you a realistic timeline once we know what we are dealing with.
