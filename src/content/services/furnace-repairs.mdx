When your furnace stops working right, the questions start stacking up fast. Is it worth repairing? Should you replace it? Can it wait or does someone need to come today?

JT's starts with the problem, not a sales pitch. Tell us what the furnace is doing and we will help you sort out the next step.

## Common furnace problems we can look at

- Furnace will not start or keeps shutting off
- Cold air, weak airflow, or uneven heat through the home
- Unusual noises, burning smells, or a system that feels unsafe
- Short cycling or a furnace that runs constantly but cannot keep up
- Thermostat, ignition, blower motor, limit switch, and flame sensor issues
- Pilot light problems or electrical control failures

## What to expect when you call

We will ask a few questions about what is happening, when it started, and how urgent the situation is. That helps us understand whether it is something you can monitor for a day or two or whether someone should come sooner.

Once we look at the furnace, we will explain what we found and talk through the practical options. Repair if it makes sense. Replace if the system is done. Keep an eye on it if that is the honest answer.

## When repair may not be the right call

Sometimes the furnace is old enough or the repair cost is high enough that replacement makes more sense. If that is the case, we will tell you why and help you talk through replacement timing and options.

You decide what fits. We are here to give you a straight answer so you can make an informed call.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my furnace needs repair or replacement?
It depends on the age of the furnace, the cost of repair, and how reliable the system has been. If the furnace is older and repairs are stacking up, replacement may make more sense. JT's will explain what we found and help you talk through the practical options before any work begins.

### What causes a furnace to stop working?
Common causes include thermostat issues, ignition or pilot light problems, dirty filters restricting airflow, blower motor failures, limit switch malfunctions, and electrical control issues. We will help you figure out what is happening and what it means.

### Can I wait a day or two if the furnace is not working?
It depends on the situation. If the home is still holding some heat and the weather is not severe, you may be able to wait for a scheduled appointment. If the temperature is dropping fast or the home is already cold, let us know so we can talk through urgency and timing.

### How long does a furnace repair usually take?
It depends on what needs attention. Some repairs can be handled in an hour or two. Others may require parts or additional diagnostic work. We will give you a realistic idea once we look at the system.

### Do you service all furnace brands?
JT's works on most residential furnace brands and models. If you know the brand and age of your system, let us know when you call. If not, we can sort that out when we look at it.

### What areas do you cover for furnace repair?
JT's serves Grand Rapids, Saranac, Lowell, Kentwood, Wyoming, Ada, Walker, Rockford, Greenville, Portland, Hudsonville, and surrounding areas in West Michigan.
