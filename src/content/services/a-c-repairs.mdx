When your air conditioner stops working right, the stress shows up fast. JT's can help figure out what is happening and talk through repair options that make sense for your home and budget.

We start with what you noticed. Tell us when it stopped working, what changed, and how urgent it is. We will ask a few questions, look at the system, and explain what we found before any repair work begins.

## Common A/C repair calls we handle

- The outdoor unit will not start or keeps shutting off mid-cycle.
- The system runs but only blows warm air or barely cool air.
- Ice forming on the indoor coil or refrigerant lines.
- Weak airflow, uneven cooling, or rooms that never get comfortable.
- Strange noises, burning smells, or a system that feels unsafe to run.
- Thermostat not communicating with the system or showing error codes.
- Water leaking from the indoor unit or around the air handler.
- The system short cycles—starts and stops too frequently without completing a cooling cycle.

## What happens during an A/C repair visit

We will look at the outdoor condenser unit, the indoor air handler or evaporator coil, the thermostat, and the airflow through your home. We check refrigerant levels, electrical connections, the condensate drain, and the parts that help the system start and stop safely.

If something is broken, worn, or not working right, we will explain what we found and what repair makes sense. If the system is old and repair costs are stacking up, we will talk through that too. You will know the options before deciding what comes next.

## When to call about A/C repair

Call when the system stops cooling, makes a new noise, smells wrong, or just feels unreliable. If you are not sure whether it is urgent, tell us what is happening and we will help you figure out timing.

Some problems can wait a few days. Others need attention before the system causes bigger damage or quits completely. We will give you a straight answer either way.

## Repair or replacement?

Sometimes repair is the right move. Sometimes replacement makes more sense. We will not push you toward the bigger job if repair fits your situation.

If the system is newer and the repair is straightforward, fixing it usually makes sense. If the unit is older, out of parts, or facing expensive component failure, replacement might be the more practical path. We will walk through both options and let you decide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my A/C needs repair?
If the system will not start, blows warm air, makes unusual noises, freezes up, or cannot keep the home comfortable, something is wrong. Call and describe what changed. We will help you figure out the next step.

### Can you repair A/C systems in hot weather?
Yes. Service is first come, first served. If your A/C is broken and the weather is hot, let us know the urgency when you call. We will do what we can to fit you in as the schedule allows.

### What if the repair costs more than expected?
We will explain what we found and what the repair involves before any work begins. If the cost is higher than you want to spend or if replacement starts to make more sense, we will talk through your options. No surprises.

### Do you work on older A/C systems?
Yes. If parts are available and repair is practical, we will fix it. If the system is too old or parts are hard to find, we will let you know and help you talk through replacement timing.

### What if I'm not sure whether it's the A/C or something else?
That is fine. Describe what is happening—warm air, weak airflow, strange behavior, whatever you noticed. We will ask a few questions and help you sort out whether it is the A/C, the thermostat, ductwork, or something else.
