When the old A/C cannot be fixed affordably, when cooling bills are climbing, or when comfort is just not there anymore, replacement becomes the practical choice. JT's will help you understand what size and type of system fits your home, your budget, and your comfort goals.

We start by talking through what is happening now and what you need from the new system. No upselling, no manufactured urgency. Just honest guidance on what makes sense for your home.

## When replacement makes more sense than repair

- The system is old and repairs are adding up fast.
- Cooling bills are high and the home still feels uncomfortable.
- The A/C struggles during summer heat or cannot keep up with demand.
- You want to plan ahead before the system fails mid-season.
- The existing unit uses outdated refrigerant or components that are harder to service.

## What to expect from the installation process

JT's will walk through the steps so you know what is involved before committing to the work.

### Talk through your home and comfort needs
We will ask about the size of your home, problem areas, ductwork condition, and what has not worked well with the old system. That helps us recommend a cooling setup that fits.

### Compare options without the sales pitch
You will see a few choices that match your situation. We will explain the differences in efficiency, cost, and long-term comfort so you can decide what fits your priorities.

### Schedule installation when it works for you
Replacement installs are scheduled in advance. We will coordinate timing, prep work, and any questions about access or setup before the install day.

### Install the system and test it before leaving
The new A/C gets installed, connected, tested, and checked for proper operation. You will know how to use the system and what to expect as it settles in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does A/C installation take?
Most residential A/C replacements take one day. Complex installs or homes that need ductwork changes may take longer. We will give you a realistic timeframe before scheduling.

### What happens to the old A/C system?
We remove and dispose of the old equipment properly, including refrigerant recovery as required.

### Do I need to be home during installation?
It helps to be available at the start and end of the install so we can answer questions, show you the new system, and make sure everything is working correctly.

### Will the new A/C work with my existing thermostat?
In many cases, yes. If your thermostat is older or incompatible, we will let you know and explain options before the install.

### What if I need A/C now but want to wait on replacement?
If repair is still an option and makes sense financially, we will talk through that first. You do not have to replace unless it is the practical choice.

### Can I get an estimate before deciding?
Yes. Reach out with details about your home and current system. We will talk through what replacement would involve and help you understand costs before any work is scheduled.
