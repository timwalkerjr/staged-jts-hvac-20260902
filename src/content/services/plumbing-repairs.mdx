Plumbing problems do not wait for a convenient time. A leak under the sink, a drain that will not clear, or a fixture that stopped working can turn into a bigger mess if you ignore it.

JT's starts with what is happening, not a sales pitch. Tell us the symptoms and we will help you decide whether it is a repair, a replacement, or something that can wait a day.

## Common plumbing problems we can look at

- Leaking faucets, supply lines, or shutoff valves
- Clogged sinks, tubs, or slow drains
- Toilets that run, leak, or will not flush right
- Fixture replacement when a repair is not worth it
- Water issues tied to a water heater or nearby piping
- Small residential plumbing jobs that do not need a whole crew

## What to expect when you call

We will ask what you are seeing, where it is happening, and how urgent it is. That helps us know whether it can wait for a scheduled visit or whether someone should come sooner.

Once we look at it, we will explain what we found and talk through the practical options. Repair if it makes sense. Replace the fixture if that is cleaner. Tell you if it is outside the scope of a one-person visit.

## When it may need more than a service call

Some plumbing issues are bigger than a same-day repair — hidden leaks in walls, sewer-line problems, or work that needs a specialist. If that is the case, we will tell you straight and help you understand the next step.

You decide what fits. We are here to give you a clear answer so you can make an informed call.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you handle emergency plumbing leaks?
Yes, when the schedule allows. If water is actively leaking and causing damage, call me and I will fit you in as soon as I can. For a burst pipe flooding the home, shut off the water at the main if you can do it safely.

### Can you replace a faucet or toilet?
Yes. If a fixture is worn out or the repair cost is close to replacement, we can talk through swapping it for a new one.

### Do you do whole-house re-pipes or sewer lines?
Those larger jobs are not the typical one-person service call. If that is what the home needs, I will tell you honestly and help you understand the next step.

### Are you licensed and insured for plumbing?
Yes — fully licensed and insured in Michigan. Happy to share paperwork before any job.
