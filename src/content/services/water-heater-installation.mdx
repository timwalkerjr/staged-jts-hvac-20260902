Replacing a water heater is stressful when you need it done quickly. But rushing the choice can mean overpaying, undersizing, or missing better options.

JT's will help you talk through what happened, what you need, and what replacement path makes sense before installation begins.

## When installation makes more sense than repair

- The tank is leaking or showing corrosion around the base or fittings.
- The water heater is more than a decade old and starting to fail.
- Hot water runs out too fast for how many people use it.
- You want to plan replacement before an urgent failure forces a fast decision.
- Repair costs are climbing and the tank is nearing the end of its practical life.

## What to think about before choosing a new tank

Not all water heaters are the same size or type. The right choice depends on how the home uses hot water.

### Household size and usage
A larger family or a home with high hot water demand needs more capacity. A smaller household can often work with a smaller, less expensive tank.

### Fuel type and hookup
Most replacements match the existing fuel type—gas or electric. Switching can add cost and complexity, but it is possible if the situation calls for it.

### Space and venting
The new tank needs to fit the space and work with the existing venting setup. JT's will check clearances and code requirements before installation.

### Recovery time and first-hour rating
How fast the tank reheats after heavy use matters. We will help you match the first-hour rating to realistic demand so you are not left short during morning or evening routines.

## What happens during installation

Once you decide on a replacement, JT's will handle the removal, installation, and hookup work.

- Drain and disconnect the old water heater.
- Remove the old tank and prepare the space for the new unit.
- Install the new water heater, connect water lines, gas or electric hookups, and venting.
- Check for leaks, test the system, and confirm it is heating properly.
- Walk you through basic operation and maintenance before leaving.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a water heater installation take?
Most standard tank replacements take a few hours. The timeline depends on the condition of the old hookups, the space, and whether any code updates are needed.

### Can I upgrade to a larger tank than I have now?
Often yes, if the space and hookups support it. We will check clearances, venting, and fuel capacity before recommending a larger size.

### Should I consider a tankless water heater instead?
Tankless units can work well for some homes, but they require different hookups, venting, and often more upfront cost. JT's can talk through whether tankless makes sense for your situation.

### What if the old water heater is in a tight space?
We will look at the space during the estimate. If access is limited, we will figure out the best removal and installation path before starting work.

### Do you handle permit and code requirements?
Yes. JT's will handle any permits required and make sure the installation meets local code.

### What happens to the old water heater?
We remove the old tank and dispose of it properly as part of the installation.

### How do I know what size water heater I need?
We will ask about household size, hot water usage, and any complaints with the current setup. That helps match the tank size to realistic demand without oversizing.

### Can I schedule installation in advance?
Yes. If the water heater is still working but nearing the end of its life, you can schedule installation at a time that fits your calendar.
