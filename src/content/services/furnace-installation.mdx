Choosing a new furnace means sorting out a lot of questions at once. Size, efficiency, ductwork fit, timing, and budget all matter. JT's can help you talk through those details without pushing you toward the most expensive option.

We will look at your home, listen to your comfort concerns, and explain what different furnace choices actually mean for your heating bills and comfort level.

## When homeowners ask about new furnace installation

- The current furnace is old and repairs are becoming frequent or costly.
- You are planning other home work and want the furnace handled at the same time.
- The home has comfort issues that a properly sized system might solve.
- You want to understand efficiency options before deciding what to buy.
- The furnace failed and replacement is the practical next step.

## What we will talk through before installation

Furnace installation is not one-size-fits-all. The right system depends on your home's layout, insulation, ductwork condition, and what you need from the equipment.

### System sizing
An oversized furnace cycles too often and wears out faster. An undersized system cannot keep up when it gets cold. We will look at your home and help you choose a furnace that fits the actual heating load.

### Efficiency levels
Higher efficiency furnaces cost more upfront but use less gas over time. We will explain the difference between standard and high-efficiency models so you can decide what makes sense for your situation and budget.

### Ductwork and airflow
If the ductwork is leaking, undersized, or poorly balanced, a new furnace will not fix comfort problems. We will let you know if duct improvements should be part of the conversation.

### Installation timing
Fall and early winter are busy. If your furnace is still limping along, scheduling the install during a slower period can give you more flexibility. If it is dead, we will work with your timeline.

## What happens during a furnace install

The install process depends on whether the new furnace is a direct swap or if ductwork, venting, or other components need adjustment.

We will remove the old equipment, position and connect the new furnace, check venting and gas connections, test the system, and walk you through the basics of operating and maintaining the new unit.

If anything unexpected comes up during the install, we will explain it before moving forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I need a new furnace or just a repair?
It depends on the age of the system, the cost of the repair, and whether the furnace has been reliable. If the unit is old and repairs are adding up, replacement often makes more sense. We will talk through what we find and help you decide.

### How long does a furnace install take?
Most residential furnace installations take one day. If ductwork modifications or other complications come up, it may take longer. We will let you know the timeline before we start.

### What furnace size do I need?
Furnace size depends on your home's square footage, insulation, ductwork, and heating needs. We will evaluate your home and recommend the right size. Bigger is not always better.

### Should I get a high-efficiency furnace?
High-efficiency furnaces use less gas, which can lower your heating bills over time. Whether the higher upfront cost makes sense depends on how long you plan to stay in the home and how much you heat. We will explain the difference so you can choose what fits.

### Can you install a furnace in the winter?
Yes. If your furnace fails during cold weather, we will work with you to schedule the install as quickly as the situation allows. Temporary heat options may be needed depending on timing.

### Do I need to replace my air conditioner at the same time?
Not always, but if the A/C is old or uses an outdoor unit that matches the furnace blower, it may make sense to plan both at once. We will let you know if that is the case for your setup.

### What if my ductwork needs work?
We will check the ductwork during the evaluation. If there are issues that will affect comfort or system performance, we will talk through what needs attention and why.
